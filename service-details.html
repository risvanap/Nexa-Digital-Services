How Nexa Facilitates Your Application

ലളിതമായ 4 ഘട്ടങ്ങളിലൂടെ സർട്ടിഫിക്കറ്റ് സ്വന്തമാക്കാം

1
Document & Eligibility Check

Our trained operator scrutinizes salary proofs, ration card categories, and address consistency before portal entry.

2
e-District Portal Upload & Acknowledgement

Direct submission to the Kerala Revenue gateway. You get an immediate SMS with the Govt Application Reference Number.

3
Village Officer Field Clearance Tracking

Nexa constantly tracks your file's desk status at the local Village Office to ensure timely report generation.

4
Digital Certificate Delivery

Receive the QR-coded, digitally signed certificate on WhatsApp, download link via SMS, or collect a high-gsm print from our center.
